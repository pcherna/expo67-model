## 3. Canadian Centre for Architecture

For research use, images taken from the CCA website are free, and a high-resolution image costs CAD 25. Expect an answer within a week and the images 4 to 8 weeks later. Ask for study-room access at ref@cca.qc.ca, with 2 weeks' notice.
