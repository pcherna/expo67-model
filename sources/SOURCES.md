## Photo and film sources (pilot)

The pilot photos and films are listed in `data/pilot/SOURCES.md`, with their licences. In short: 10 Wikimedia Commons photos (CC BY-SA, mostly by Laurent Bélanger) and 6 Internet Archive films. Only the 1967 Universal newsreel among the films is public domain.

Facts used from the web:
- Netherlands pavilion lattice module: "A three-foot cube with a diagonal connection forms the base module" ([EGM](https://egm.nl/en/architects/projects/dutch-pavilion-world-expo-1967/)).

## Archives

Drafts of the requests are in `../docs/archive-requests.md`.

- **Library and Archives Canada:** Canadian Corporation for the 1967 World Exhibition fonds (photo example e001096672) and the site plan RG71M 85603-17-011. The LAC website could not be reached on 2026-11-20.
- **Archives de Montréal:** 1966 and 1969 aerial photos (VM97-3) and Expo 67 photos (for example VM94-EX137-0224). Access was "temporairement interrompu" on 2026-11-20. Catalogue: https://archivesdemontreal.ica-atom.org/.
- **Canadian Centre for Architecture:** [May Cutler collection on Expo 67](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/archives/379544/may-cutler-collection-on-expo-67), reference CD047, 578 digitised items. Copies through reproductions@cca.qc.ca, and images from the CCA website are free for research.

## Candidate sources not yet used

- Library and Archives Canada site blueprint, 22 Jan 1967, with full legend (RG71M 85603-17-011). This is likely more planimetric than the souvenir map.
- 1966 aerial photos of Montreal on Données Québec (`vmtl-vues-aeriennes-de-montreal-1958-1975`).
- Expo 67 Museum: a 1200 DPI scan of the TD Bank map, available on request.
